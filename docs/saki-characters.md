# Saki - Mahjong Anime - Characters and Superpowers

## Anime Overview

**Saki / 咲-Saki-** - mahjong school sports anime.

**Origin:** Manga by Ritz Kobayashi, serialized in Square Enix's *Young Gangan* since Feb 2006. Ongoing.

**Anime:**
1. **Saki (2009)** - 25 eps. Studios: Gonzo eps 1-14, Picture Magic eps 15-25. Aired Apr 6 - Sep 28, 2009 on TV Tokyo.
2. **Saki: Achiga-hen episode of Side-A (2012)** - 12 eps + 4 specials. Studio Gokumi. Spin-off / side story.
3. **Saki: Zenkoku-hen / The Nationals (2014)** - 13 eps. Studio Gokumi.
OVAs, live-action TV series (2016-2017) and film (2017) also exist.

Stream: Crunchyroll. MAL score ~7.46 for S1.

**Plot:**
Saki Miyanaga, a high school freshman, hates mahjong because her family punished her whether she won or lost. She learned to play to finish at exactly +/- zero - harder than winning.

Invited by friend Kyotaro Suga to Kiyosumi High's mahjong club, she meets national middle-school champion Nodoka Haramura. Together with club president Hisa Takei, Mako Someya and Yuuki Kataoka, they aim for the high school national championship in Japanese riichi mahjong.

Rival: Teru Miyanaga, Saki's older sister, ace of Shiraitodai High.

**Setting:** Mahjong is a major competitive school sport. Girls have supernatural abilities/luck powers affecting tiles.

In *Saki* they are called monsters - luck / sensory powers tied to mahjong.

## Characters and Superpowers

### Kiyosumi High
- **Saki Miyanaga**: +/- zero score control + `rinshan kaihou` - wins off dead-wall after kan, senses kan replacements in advance. Multiple kans / san-kantsu streaks.
- **Nodoka Haramura**: Digital / Nodocchi mode - online-speed draws, sees board mathematically, immune to visual powers like stealth. Needs Etopen penguin to enter state.
- **Yuuki Kataoka**: East-round specialist - huge leads in East, weak in South. Boosted by tacos / taco-power.
- **Mako Someya**: Field memory - removes glasses to recall all past discards / table flow from grandfather's parlor experience.
- **Hisa Takei**: Hell-waits / jigoku-machi - wins on 1-tile-left waits, intimidation + fake riichi to force exhaustive draws.
- **Kyotaro Suga**: No power - only male club member.

### Ryuumonbuchi (Nagano)
- **Koromo Amae**: `haitei raoyue` - wins on last tile, suppresses opponents tenpai, senses tenpai/value. Stronger near full moon.
- **Touka Ryuumonbuchi**: Flood control / chisui - controls flow.
- **Momoko Touyoko**: Stealth / negative presence - opponents can't see/hear her discards, silent riichi. Countered by digital players like Nodoka.
- **Jun Inoue**: Naki improvement - next draw after chi/pon/kan improves hand.
- **Hajime Kunihiro**: Defensive support.

### Tsuruga / Kazekoshi (Nagano)
- **Kaori Senoo (Tsuruga)**: Beginner's luck + yakuman specialist
- **Mihoko Fukuji (Kazekoshi Captain)**: Field awareness - reads opponent hands / table state
- **Kana Ikeda (Kazekoshi)**: Comeback - big haneman-sanbaiman hands when points <10k
- **Miharu Yoshitome (Kazekoshi)**: Danger warning - senses dangerous discards / furikomi prevention

### Achiga Girls Academy (Nara - Side-A protagonists)
- **Kuro Matsumi (Vanguard)**: Dora bomb - draws all dora every hand, must keep dora in hand
- **Yuu Matsumi (2nd)**: Red-tile gathering - draws red-marked / manzu + chun tiles, creative waits
- **Shizuno Takakamo (Captain)**: Dice / wall control
- **Arata Sagimori (Vice-Captain)**: Pinzu / pair listening, later copies Harue style
- **Ako Atarashi (Middle)**: Calling timing
- **Harue Akado (Coach)**: Minute observation - boosts team strength quickly

### Senriyama Girls (North Osaka)
- **Toki Onjouji**: 1-turn / multi-turn precognition - sees future draws, `riichi ippatsu tsumo` unless turn order changed by call
- **Ryuuka Shimizudani**: Borrowed precognition + analysis from Toki
- **Sera Eguchi / Izumi Nijou / Hiroko Funakubo**: Support / analysis / defense

### Himematsu High (South Osaka)
- **Kyouko Suehara (Captain)**: Speed specialist + strategist / info analysis
- **Manami Ue-Shige / Jou Yuasa**: 789-tiles specialist
- **Hiroe Atago / Kinue Atago**: Ace / defense

### Shiraitodai (West Tokyo - champions)
- **Teru Miyanaga (Vanguard)**: Shoumakyou mirror + escalating streak - surveys ability turn 1, then wins ever-more-expensive hands until stopped. Tsumo-only streak to 9-gates yakuman.
- **Sumire Hirose (2nd)**: Sniping / nerai-uchi - targeted direct hits, ToraHime combo with Teru
- **Takami Shibuya (Middle)**: Harvest Time - first discard of each hand returns on all-last
- **Seiko Matano (Vice)**: Naki / fishing specialist - wins within 5 turns after 3 calls
- **Awai Oohoshi (Captain, 1st-year monster)**: Cursed start - opponents start 5-6 shanten, unlimited double-riichi, successor to Teru

### Miyamori Girls (Iwate)
- **Toyone Anetai**: Rokuyou - Senbu pursuit-riichi behind-you win, Tomobiki 1-tile-left win, Shakkou dora calling
- **Shiromi Kosegawa**: Mayohiga - misdirection / unmotivated dealer
- **Aislinn Wishart**: Draw ideal win - visualizes winning shape
- **Sai Usuzawa**: Target nullification vs enemy ability
- **Kurumi Kurakari**: Suppression / defensive minimization

### Eisui Girls (Shimane) / Shindouji (Fukuoka) / Rinkai (East Tokyo)
- **Komaki Jindai (Eisui)**: Possession / descent - god-tier draw, one of 3 demons named by Fujita-pro
- **Hatsumi Usuzumi (Eisui)**: Demon gate / Kimon - north-round power + Big Four Winds yakuman
- **Kasumi Iwato (Eisui)**: Descent absolute one-gate
- **Kirame Hanada (Shindouji)**: Tobi-prevention (prevents bust-out)
- **Rumi Shirouzu / Himeko Tsuruta (Shindouji)**: Reservation front / back
- **Mei-Hua Que / Choe Myeonghwa (Rinkai)**: Wind-tiles specialist
- **Megan Davin (Rinkai)**: Sashi-uma betting specialist
- **Nelly Virsaladze (Rinkai)**: Wave-riding luck
